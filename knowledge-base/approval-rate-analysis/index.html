Approval Rate Analysis

Whenever customers apply for a loan, they are either approved or rejected based on policy. Changes in policy or incoming population change the approval rate — so it must be tracked continuously, and every movement must be located in a segment.

Approval rate is a function of incoming population and underwriting policy.

Dataset: synthetic — 7 days of decisioned loan applications (10–16 Aug 2024), created only to demonstrate the method. Approval rate = Approved ÷ (Approved + Rejected).

One dataset, three questions: Who is coming in? Who is getting approved? And what is the approval rate inside each segment?

Approval rate by segment

The method, in one view

  • - Monitor the overall approval rate daily.
  • - Never conclude from the overall rate alone — split by segment.
  • - Watch reject reasons for policy, population and system signals.
  • - Compare incoming vs approved distribution to see what policy is selecting.
  • - Read every rate with its volume.
