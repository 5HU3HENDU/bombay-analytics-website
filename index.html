Our Aim

Our aim is to empower lending analytics teams & community by providing a platform where people can come together and network, discuss ideas, trends, methods and best practices used in their day-to-day work!

  • Niche community
  • Real conversations
  • Right people
  • Career growth
  • No noise
  • No generic finance talk

This is for you

If you work in credit risk underwriting, risk analytics, collections analytics, lending portfolio management or lending strategy at a bank, NBFC, fintech or a credit bureau!

Why are we doing this?

Credit risk domain sits at the intersection of data science, statistics, finance and macro and micro-economic policies and it's evolving fast.

Yet most knowledge stays inside company walls, hiring happens through cold applications, and professionals rarely know who else is solving the same problems. We're trying to change that.

  • - To bring people closer in the niche of credit analytics.
  • - The domain is evolving and sits at the intersection of tech, analytics and economic trends.
  • - Connecting right people together will result in better portfolio & risk management.
  • - Networking will lead to better hiring and career growth for everyone!
"That's why we are building this platform around community of people belonging to the lending analytics domain!"
