Why are we doing this?

Credit risk sits at the intersection of data science, statistics, finance and macro & micro-economic policy — and it's evolving fast. Yet most knowledge stays inside company walls, hiring happens through cold applications, and professionals rarely know who else is solving the same problems. We're trying to change that.

  • To bring people closer in the niche of credit analytics.
  • The domain is evolving, at the intersection of tech, analytics and economic trends.
  • Connecting the right people leads to better portfolio & risk management.
  • Networking leads to better hiring and career growth for everyone.
That's why we are building this platform around a community of people from the lending analytics domain.
